How mushrooms are grown

Mushroom cultivation
step by step.

A general overview of how mushrooms are grown, from preparing the substrate to harvest.

  1. 01 / PREPARE

    Substrate preparation

    Pellets or paddy straw are soaked and mixed with supplements like rice bran, gypsum, chalk powder and lime, then treated to reduce contamination.

  2. 02 / SPAWN

    Bagging, spawning & incubation

    The cooled substrate is mixed with spawn, filled into PP covers and sealed. The bags rest in a dark room while white mycelium spreads through them.

  3. 03 / GROW

    Growing & monitoring

    In the grow room, humidity, temperature and fresh air are managed as tiny mushroom pins form and grow into full clusters.

  4. 04 / HARVEST

    Harvesting & packing

    Mushrooms are picked at the right size and packed with care for fresh supply. The same bags can give more than one harvest (flush).
